# Kia Vehicle Card

A dashboard card for Kia, Hyundai and Genesis vehicles that use the [Kia UVO / Hyundai Bluelink integration](https://github.com/Hyundai-Kia-Connect/kia_uvo).

- Fuel, EV and 12V battery, range, doors, windows, location and service countdown
- Alerts for fluids, lamps, tires and the 12V battery, shown only while active
- Climate presets, lock and unlock with a confirmation tap, and quick actions
- Shows only what your vehicle reports, so nothing on the card is guessed

After installing, add the card to a dashboard and pick your vehicle in its editor.

See the [README](https://github.com/auadix/ha-kia-card#readme) for screenshots and every option.
